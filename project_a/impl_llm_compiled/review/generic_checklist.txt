1. round_nearest_ten() is wrong: amount.quantize(Decimal('10')) rounds to the nearest RUPEE, not to the nearest ten rupees. Sec 288A/288B: first drop paise (floor to whole rupees), then round to the nearest multiple of 10 where a last digit of 5 or more rounds UP. Example: 700005 -> 700010, 700004 -> 700000, 59798.40 -> 59800.
2. The trace does not add up. The final "tax" line must be the ROUNDING ADJUSTMENT (rounded tax minus unrounded tax), not the full tax payable; likewise the Sec 288A income line must be the adjustment (rounded total income minus unrounded), not the full total income. After the fix: sum of income amounts == total_income and sum of tax amounts == tax_payable exactly.
3. Slab tax is one trace line; emit one tax line per slab band so each rupee is attributable to a band.
4. Surcharge marginal relief is merged into the surcharge line. Emit the full surcharge and then a separate negative "marginal relief" line.
5. Remove the unused function calculate_surcharge_and_marginal_relief (dead code containing `if True else`).
6. round_nearest_ten() says "floor to whole rupees" in its docstring but uses ROUND_HALF_UP, so 636344.80 becomes 636345 and then 636350. Sec 288B says paise are IGNORED: use ROUND_FLOOR to get whole rupees (636344), then round to ten (636340).
7. Use ONE consistent slab representation for every regime: a list of (lower, upper_or_None, rate) where each rate applies to income above `lower` up to `upper`.
8. Surcharge marginal relief: use ONLY the highest threshold the income exceeds. Compare (tax + surcharge) with (tax on income equal to that threshold + surcharge on it at the rate that applies AT the threshold, i.e. the LOWER rate, which is 0% at the first threshold of 50 lakh) + (income - threshold). Relief = the excess, if positive. At exactly the threshold no surcharge applies (income must EXCEED it).
