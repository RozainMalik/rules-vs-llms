1. round_nearest_ten() is wrong: amount.quantize(Decimal('10')) rounds to the nearest RUPEE, not to the nearest ten rupees. Sec 288A/288B: first drop paise (floor to whole rupees), then round to the nearest multiple of 10 where a last digit of 5 or more rounds UP. Example: 700005 -> 700010, 700004 -> 700000, 59798.40 -> 59800. 138 of 281 test cases are off by a few rupees because of this.
2. The trace does not add up. The final "tax" line must be the ROUNDING ADJUSTMENT (rounded tax minus unrounded tax), not the full tax payable; likewise the Sec 288A income line must be the adjustment (rounded total income minus unrounded), not the full total income. After the fix: sum of income amounts == total_income and sum of tax amounts == tax_payable exactly.
3. Slab tax is one trace line; emit one tax line per slab band so each rupee is attributable to a band.
4. Surcharge marginal relief is merged into the surcharge line. Emit the full surcharge and then a separate negative "marginal relief" line.
5. Remove the unused function calculate_surcharge_and_marginal_relief (dead code containing `if True else`).
6. (round 2) round_nearest_ten() says "floor to whole rupees" in its docstring but uses ROUND_HALF_UP, so 636344.80 becomes 636345 and then 636350. Sec 288B says paise are IGNORED: use ROUND_FLOOR to get whole rupees (636344), then round to ten (636340). 5 of 281 test cases are Rs 10 too high because of this.
